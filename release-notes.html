25 October 2026

0.1.2: Linux

WindowsLinux Nine applications on Windows, five on Linux.

The first Linux release

Vex, Pix, MainFrame, Calculator and VexelRay Inspector, for 64-bit Linux on X11 or XWayland. One line installs them for your user under ~/.local, with menu entries, Open With file types, and one command to uninstall: curl -fsSL https://sibarum.github.io/install.sh | sh

Record any window

Every application has a camera button in its title bar that records the window to video, AV1 in Matroska.

Motion, on by default

Things enter and leave with motion, and zoom glides, across the whole window. Reduced motion follows your system's setting. When frames run slow, an application offers simpler graphics.

Applications

  • MainFrame: terminals run on a pseudo-terminal on every platform. On Linux it offers your login shell first, then bash, zsh, fish and sh. Environment scripts work in bash and zsh too.
  • MainFrame: Ctrl+C copies a selection, and interrupts when there is none. A passthrough switch hands every key to the shell.
  • Vex: a run uses a pseudo-terminal on every platform.
  • VexelRay Inspector: a component graph of the running app; the tape, to pause and look back; what the app draws with; and a tachometer of frames drawn against the display's refresh rate.
  • Vexplore: the suggestion rail splits folders from suggestions with a divider you can drag.
  • Calculator: points off the real line are plotted, with the imaginary part as a curve of its own.
  • Pix: on Linux, with FFmpeg for video and sound. Pictures get an automatic backdrop.
  • Rigid Sim: falls back to the CPU on a graphics card with no compute queue of its own.
  • Every application: a filled-in About window.

Framework

  • Linux windowing over X11, presented through a Vulkan swapchain. A window keeps its size and place when it moves to another monitor.
  • Audio: the codec plays MP3, AAC, FLAC, Vorbis, Opus and WAV, with one-knob compression and a night mode.
  • Recording is a separate, encoder-only FFmpeg, so it adds 5.5 MiB, not a full FFmpeg.
  • A split pane can show one pane alone. Icons are baked at build time, and an application's own set extends the suite's.
  • Single-channel SDF font atlases, as an alternative to MSDF.
  • Key maps run actions in order and can be paused.
  • A check of the stack at startup, and a @Trace weaver.
  • Intrabuf: nanobuf and nanotemplate, a reader and a writer in one shell line. See Nanobuf golf.
  • Atchung: cause correlation, and a trace lane for probes.
  • One step installs the whole stack for development: bootstrap.sh.

Installers

  • Linux installers for every application and the suite: install, update and uninstall, each checked by SHA-256.
  • Desktop entries are named so docks match them to their window. A rename keeps file-type defaults and moves GNOME dock pins.

Not on Linux yet: Vexplore, Fluid Sim, Rigid Sim and Maistro. Wayland works through XWayland, and every window draws at 100%, whatever the desktop's scaling.

20 October 2026

0.1.1: nine applications, and undo everywhere

Windows Maistro and VexelRay Inspector join the suite.

Two new applications

Maistro, a desktop client for Claude Code with a mind map beside the conversation, and VexelRay Inspector, which attaches to a running app and shows its frame loop. Both are optional, like the simulations.

Undo, everywhere

Every window has one undo history. Every control that holds a value records into it. Ctrl+Z first shows you where the change was, then reverses it there. An undo that can't apply says why. Ctrl+Y redoes.

Pix plays video

With sound, volume and mute that are remembered, and a preview frame on the seek bar. Animated GIF, WebP and APNG play like movies. A double-click goes full screen.

Applications

  • Maistro (new): inline Allow and Deny for tool permissions; conversations are saved and resumed (Ctrl+N, Ctrl+O); and a branch from a node of the map merges back.
  • VexelRay Inspector (new): attach to an app chosen from a picker, or by launching it. Stalls are caught, and history is kept per build.
  • Vexplore: remote folders over SFTP, through your own ssh. Moves, copies, archives and deletes can be undone and redone.
  • Vexplore: two folders side by side, with drag between them; an Open menu in the title bar, with New host; videos previewed in the dock and opened in Pix; and Open in Windows Explorer.
  • Vex: a Run menu (F5) with a console; a formatted Markdown view (Ctrl+Shift+V); an undo history per file; and file-type icons.
  • Vex: Scratches, notes that belong to no project. Preferences, kept per project, with a light theme, word wrap and indexing. Open in MainFrame on folders.
  • Pix: touchpad pan, pinch and twist, and every key and gesture can be rebound. Video files can open in Pix.
  • MainFrame: zoom with Ctrl+= and Ctrl+-. Environment scripts per workspace and per tab, edited in Vex. Every command is an action you can rebind.
  • Calculator: answers are typeset and simplified. A plot pops out into an explorer you can pan and zoom from the keyboard, and each order of 0/ω is plotted as its own curve.
  • Rigid Sim: boxes as well as spheres, a pile of 1,000 that comes to rest, and rolling resistance. Fluid Sim: slime.
  • Every application: offers its own updates, has an icon in the notification area, and installs a THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt beside it.

Framework

  • Your key bindings: rebind any key and it simply takes effect. Touchpad gestures bind like keys.
  • Automation: one socket drives every window, and remote control and diagnostics are separate sockets. Restart in debug mode from the title bar's plug. ottermate gained list, await, keydown/keyup and hide/show, and settle names what keeps the loop busy.
  • Background runs: an app a coding agent starts draws for real but stays out of the way, never taking focus, and the person at the desk can end it from the notification area.
  • A flight recorder of recent frames, with frames worst and why.
  • Borderless full screen. Drawing works on dzn, Vulkan over Direct3D 12.
  • Colour fades, press ripples and gliding tab underlines. Drag between lists.
  • A mesh technique for polygon models, and streamed textures for live video frames.
  • Kronometer: tempos that glide, in exact ratios.
  • Every module is stamped with the build it came from. A developer guide.

Installer

  • One installer window, with a box for each application and option, progress and a summary.
  • Optional applications start unticked. --install-everything and --do-not-prompt-me.
  • System requirements are checked first, and diagnose.ps1 writes a report.
  • An install is a transaction: it backs up and rolls back. Downloads run four at a time, resume, retry, and are cached by SHA-256.
  • An app manager in the Start menu.
13 October 2026

0.1.0: the first release

Windows Seven applications, installed with one line of PowerShell.

Vexplore, Vex, Pix, MainFrame, Calculator, Fluid Sim and Rigid Sim, each installed for your user only, every download checked by SHA-256, with Open with entries for the file types Vex and Pix read. What they gained in the weeks before it:

Applications

  • Vexplore: the suggestion rail, and actions where a modifier key says what you mean. An image preview dock, and pictures open in Pix. A git status column inside a repository, and Open MainFrame Here.
  • Vex: takes its name. Go to declaration (Ctrl+Enter), in your project and its libraries, decompiled when there are no sources. Open in Vexplore. Each project root keeps its own tabs.
  • Pix (new): a picture viewer whose window is the picture, sharp when you look closely, with scaling settings and filters.
  • MainFrame: rebuilt on the framework's template, with tabs, workspaces (Ctrl+O), settings, and --dir, --workspace and --insert-path.
  • Calculator (new): evaluates in cott-engine's traction algebras, and past lines come back to the entry.
  • Fluid Sim: surface tension, temperature and buoyancy, foam and boiling, and a 3D dam break.
  • Rigid Sim: physics on its own Vulkan queue, with restitution, spin and friction.

Framework

  • A frame is drawn only when something changed, not on every wake.
  • A watchdog and stall detection for each lane. A blocking cycle of sends is a compile error. A compute queue can be lent to one component.
  • On Windows, every window presents through DXGI, and a resize reallocates nothing.
  • Shadelets: an uber-shader each application composes, with looks and styles.
  • Baked font families, with bold and italic spans. A single focus ring drawn by the renderer. Board and deck cards, slider detents, and word and line selection.
  • Intrabuf, new: generated binary codecs with a text form. Kronometer: the handoff, and dilated time.
  • The template writes a working text editor. The build fails if LWJGL appears anywhere.
  • Lean proofs that the fluid's particle-to-grid scatter is deterministic.
